WeightFlip — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026 · App: WeightFlip (package com.grupocodeit.weightflip), version 1.1

Looking for the rules of use? See the Terms of Use below.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in. Your conversions, favorites and settings stay on your phone (and in your Android backup, if it's on) unless you share or export them. We never receive them.
  • The app is free and shows ads. Google's ad, analytics, crash-reporting and performance tools collect some device data, such as your Advertising ID and an approximate location based on your IP address.
  • The app sends Firebase Analytics a few usage events, such as "a conversion was made" and which units you used. They never include the values you enter or the results.
  • Watching one rewarded ad unlocks Premium until midnight. No payment, no account.
  • Where consent is required (for example, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you're asked first and can change your choice anytime in Settings > Privacy Options.
  • We don't sell your data.

Who we are

WeightFlip is developed by GRUPO CODEIT ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your data: grupocodeit.business@gmail.com. Website: grupocodeit.github.io.

What the app keeps on your device

The app has no servers of its own. It keeps the following in its private storage on your phone, and we never receive it. If Android backup is on, part of it may also be copied to your Google account (see Backup and moving to a new phone):

  • Your conversion history. When you stop typing for a moment, the conversion is saved with the value you entered, both units, the result, the conversion formula, and the date and time. The History screen shows your latest 100; older ones stay saved until you clear your history.
  • Your favorites: the unit pairs you star, with the date you added them.
  • Your preferences: theme, decimal places, your default units (the last pair you used) and whether you've finished the welcome screens.
  • Premium: when you last earned it. That's the date and time, the local date, and a reading of the phone's internal clock (the time since it was turned on), so the app can tell if the clock or the date has been changed.
  • Ad pacing: when the last app-open ad was shown, so those ads stay at least 4 hours apart.
  • Your consent choices, saved by Google's consent tool so you aren't asked every time. Google's ad and analytics tools send your consent signals to Google with their requests.
  • Shared export files in the app's cache (see Exporting your history).

Copying and sharing

Copy puts a result or the conversion formula on your phone's clipboard. Share opens Android's share menu with the conversion formula as text (for example, "1 kg = 2.20462262 lb"), and you choose the app it goes to. We never receive what you copy or share, and the app doesn't know which app you chose.

Exporting your history

With Premium, you can export your latest 100 conversions as a CSV or PDF file, with each value, the units, the result, the formula and the date and time.

  • Save CSV / Save PDF opens Android's file picker, and the file is saved where you choose. It's then your file; the app doesn't delete it.
  • Share CSV / Share PDF creates the file in the app's cache and opens Android's share menu. Only the app you choose gets permission to read it. These files stay in the cache until Android clears it, or until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

We never receive exported files.

Deleting your data

  • On the History screen, you can delete a single conversion or clear them all. Settings > Clear History also deletes all saved conversions.
  • Settings > Clear Favorites deletes all your favorites.
  • Clearing the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > WeightFlip > Storage) or uninstalling the app deletes everything the app keeps on your phone, including your preferences, Premium, the ad pacing data, your consent choices and cached export files. Files you saved with Save CSV or Save PDF stay where you saved them.

Backup and moving to a new phone

If Android backup is turned on, Android may save the app's data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. A direct transfer between phones can also move it. This backup is managed by Google under your account; we can't access it.

  • Included: your history, favorites, preferences and consent choices, and the files Google's SDKs keep (which can include your consent signals and identifiers such as the app instance ID).
  • Left out: Premium and the ad pacing data. So Premium never moves to another phone. Cached export files aren't included either.
  • A backup made with version 1.0 may still contain the old reward times; version 1.1 deletes them when it starts.

Analytics events

Besides what Firebase collects automatically, the app records these events. They never include the values you enter, results or any identifier; their parameters only take the fixed values shown.

  • conversion_performed: a conversion was made. from_unit, to_unit and pair: the two units (for example, KILOGRAM and POUND).
  • units_swapped: you swapped the units. from_unit, to_unit.
  • favorite_added, favorite_removed, favorite_opened: you starred, removed or opened a favorite. from_unit, to_unit (and pair when added).
  • history_cleared, favorites_cleared: you cleared your history or favorites from Settings. No parameters.
  • theme_changed, decimal_places_changed, default_units_changed: you changed a setting. The new theme, number of decimal places (1 to 15) or default units.
  • onboarding_completed, onboarding_skipped: you finished or skipped the welcome screens (page_index: the screen you skipped from).
  • premium_unlocked: you unlocked Premium with a rewarded ad. placement: where you started, premium_screen, references, export or precision.

Firebase adds its usual data to each event, such as the time, the app instance ID, device info and approximate location. What it can store depends on your consent (see Consent).

Advertising ID

Google's ad and analytics tools use your device's Advertising ID to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can delete or reset it anytime on Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device). After you delete it, apps can no longer use it for personalized ads. The ads SDK also reads an app set ID, an identifier that Google Play services gives to apps from the same developer; deleting or resetting your Advertising ID doesn't change it. The app doesn't use Android's own ad privacy features (such as ad topics and ad measurement).

Approximate location

The app doesn't ask for location permission and doesn't use GPS. Google's services may infer an approximate location (such as your country or city) from your IP address, for ad delivery, analytics, fraud prevention and to tell whether consent is required where you are.

Consent

  • If you're in a region where consent is required (for example, the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. Until your choice is known, no ads are requested and the app sets all Firebase Analytics consent types to denied.
  • If the app can't confirm your consent, it requests no ads and denies all analytics consent types. In that case, Premium can't be unlocked.
  • If you decline, the app still works. Ads may be limited or non-personalized, as Google defines them, or unavailable.
  • Firebase Analytics follows your choice through Google Consent Mode v2. When consent is denied, Google may still receive limited, cookieless signals without identifiers, as described in Google's policies. Performance Monitoring runs only if analytics is allowed; a change to that choice takes effect the next time you open the app.
  • Where consent isn't required, analytics and ad signals are allowed by default; the Advertising ID controls above still apply.
  • You can change your choice anytime in Settings > Privacy Options (shown where it applies to your region). When you do, ads already loaded are discarded and new ones are requested under your new choice.
  • Crash reporting isn't tied to consent: we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working. It can't be turned off inside the app; uninstalling the app stops it.

Premium until midnight

Conversions, all units, favorites, history, copying, sharing and 1 to 6 decimal places are free. Watching one rewarded ad unlocks Premium until midnight (your local time) on the day you watch it: no ads (no banner, no full-screen ads), up to 15 decimal places, CSV and PDF export, and the reference charts. It ends at midnight, not after 24 hours. Premium is unlocked only when Google's ad SDK confirms you earned the reward, usually by watching the ad to the end. On your phone, the app stores when you earned Premium (see What the app keeps on your device); the Premium event sent to Firebase is listed under Analytics events. If the phone's date changes, Premium ends, and setting the clock back can end it too. The next day, you can watch another ad. Rewards earned with version 1.0 aren't carried over to version 1.1.

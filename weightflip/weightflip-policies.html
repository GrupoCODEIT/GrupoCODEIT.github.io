WeightFlip — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026 · App: WeightFlip (package com.grupocodeit.weightflip), version 1.0

Looking for the rules of use? See the Terms of Use below.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in. Your conversions, favorites and settings stay on your phone (and in your Android backup, if it's on) unless you share or export them. We never receive them.
  • The app is free and shows ads. Google's ad, analytics, crash-reporting and performance tools collect some device data, such as your Advertising ID and an approximate location based on your IP address.
  • The app sends Firebase Analytics usage events, such as "a conversion was made". In this version, that event includes the units and the value you entered.
  • Watching a rewarded ad unlocks one extra feature for a limited time. No payment, no account.
  • Where consent is required (for example, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you're asked before any ad is requested.
  • We don't sell your data.

Who we are

WeightFlip is developed by GRUPO CODEIT ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your data: grupocodeit.business@gmail.com. Website: grupocodeit.github.io.

What the app keeps on your device

The app has no servers of its own. It keeps the following in its private storage on your phone, and we never receive it. If Android backup is on, it may also be copied to your Google account (see Backup and moving to a new phone):

  • Your conversion history. When you stop typing for a moment, the conversion is saved with the value you entered, both units, the result, the conversion formula, and the date and time. The History screen shows your latest 100; older ones stay saved until you clear your history.
  • Your favorites: the unit pairs you star, with the date you added them.
  • Your preferences: theme, decimal places, your default units (the last pair you used) and whether you've finished the welcome screens.
  • Rewards: when each feature you unlocked with a rewarded ad expires.
  • Your consent choices, saved by Google's consent tool so you aren't asked every time.
  • Shared export files in the app's cache (see Exporting your history).

Copying and sharing

Copy puts a result or the conversion formula on your phone's clipboard. Share opens Android's share menu with the conversion formula as text (for example, "1 kg = 2.2 lb"), and you choose the app it goes to. We never receive what you copy or share, and the app doesn't know which app you chose.

Exporting your history

When Premium Exports is unlocked, you can export your latest 100 conversions as a CSV or PDF file, with each value, the units, the result, the formula and the date and time.

  • Save CSV / Save PDF opens Android's file picker, and the file is saved where you choose. It's then your file; the app doesn't delete it.
  • Share CSV / Share PDF creates the file in the app's cache and opens Android's share menu. Only the app you choose gets permission to read it. These files stay in the cache until Android clears it, or until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

We never receive exported files.

Deleting your data

  • On the History screen, you can delete a single conversion or clear them all. Settings > Clear History also deletes all saved conversions.
  • Settings > Clear Favorites deletes all your favorites.
  • Clearing the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > WeightFlip > Storage) or uninstalling the app deletes everything the app keeps on your phone, including your preferences, reward unlocks, consent choices and cached export files. Files you saved with Save CSV or Save PDF stay where you saved them.

Backup and moving to a new phone

If Android backup is turned on, Android may save the app's data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. A direct transfer between phones can also move it. In this version, that includes your history, favorites, preferences, reward expiry times and consent choices, as well as the files Google's SDKs keep (which can include identifiers such as the app instance ID). Cached export files aren't included. This backup is managed by Google under your account; we can't access it.

Analytics events

Besides what Firebase collects automatically, the app records these events:

  • conversion_performed: a conversion was made. from_unit, to_unit, pair (the two units) and input_value (the value you entered).
  • units_swapped: you swapped the units. from_unit, to_unit.
  • favorite_added, favorite_removed, favorite_opened: you starred, removed or opened a favorite. from_unit, to_unit (and pair when added).
  • history_cleared, favorites_cleared: you cleared your history or favorites from Settings. No parameters.
  • theme_changed, decimal_places_changed, default_units_changed: you changed a setting. The new theme, number of decimal places or default units.
  • onboarding_completed, onboarding_skipped: you finished or skipped the welcome screens (page_index: the screen you skipped from).
  • reward_earned: you unlocked a feature with a rewarded ad. feature: extended_precision, export_history, reference_charts or ad_free_60min.

Firebase adds its usual data to each event, such as the time, the app instance ID, device info and approximate location.

Advertising ID

Google's ad and analytics tools use your device's Advertising ID to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can delete or reset it anytime on Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device). After you delete it, apps can no longer use it for personalized ads. The ads SDK also reads an app set ID, an identifier that Google Play services gives to apps from the same developer; deleting or resetting your Advertising ID doesn't change it. The app doesn't use Android's own ad privacy features (such as ad topics and ad measurement).

Approximate location

The app doesn't ask for location permission and doesn't use GPS. Google's services may infer an approximate location (such as your country or city) from your IP address, for ad delivery, analytics, fraud prevention and to tell whether consent is required where you are.

Consent

  • If you're in a region where consent is required (for example, the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form each time you open it, until you answer, and before any ad is requested.
  • If the app can't confirm that ads may be requested, it requests no ads. In that case, rewarded features can't be unlocked.
  • If you decline personalized ads, the app still works and may show non-personalized or limited ads, as Google defines them.
  • In this version, the app doesn't have an option to change your choice later. To be asked again, clear the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > WeightFlip > Storage) and open the app.
  • In this version, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring aren't tied to your answer in the consent form. We'll update this policy when that changes.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent: personalized ads, and storing and reading information on your device for ads. You give or refuse it in Google's consent form.
  • Legitimate interest: analytics, crash reporting and performance monitoring that help us keep the app working and improve it, fraud prevention and security, and showing non-personalized or limited ads that keep the app free.
  • Legal obligation: keeping a record of your consent choices.

Rewards

Each reward is unlocked by watching one rewarded ad, and only when Google's ad SDK confirms you earned it, usually by watching the ad to the end:

  • Extended Precision (up to 15 decimal places), Premium Exports and Reference Charts stay unlocked until 11:59 PM (your local time) on the day you watch the ad.
  • Ad-Free for 60 Minutes hides the banner and the full-screen ads between conversions for 60 minutes.

On your phone, the app stores only when each reward expires; the reward events sent to Firebase are listed under Analytics events. There's no payment or subscription.

Retention

  • Your history and favorites stay until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Your preferences, reward expiry times and consent choices stay until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Shared export files stay in the app's cache until Android clears it, or until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • If Android backup is on, the backup copy of the app's data stays in your Google account under Google's backup rules, even after you uninstall the app, and can be restored when you reinstall it. You can remove it by deleting your device's backup from your Google account.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as we need them to answer you.
  • Data collected by Google is kept under Google's own retention policies (see the links above): user-level analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, and aggregated reports that don't identify you may be kept longer; crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

Security

Data sent by these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). What the app keeps is stored in its private storage, which other apps can't read; it isn't encrypted on the device beyond Android's own protections. Other apps can read a shared export file only if you choose them in the share menu.

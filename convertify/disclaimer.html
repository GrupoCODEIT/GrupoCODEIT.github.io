How results are calculated

  • Standard factors. The app uses widely accepted conversion factors. Some units have more than one definition (for example, US and imperial gallons, or short, long and metric tons). Where the app knows several, it lists them as separate units: pick the one you need, and check that its definition matches the one your country, industry or standard requires.
  • Rounding. Results are rounded to the number of decimal places you choose in Settings. Rounding, and repeated rounding in conversion chains, can make small differences add up.
  • No context. The app doesn't take into account temperature, pressure, density, local regulations or any other conditions that can change a real measurement. Cooking conversions, for example, assume standard measures, not the weight of a specific ingredient.

Don't use it as your only source for

  • Medicine and health: medication doses, clinical measurements or anything that affects the health of people or animals.
  • Engineering and construction: structural design, loads, electrical installations or technical drawings.
  • Aviation and navigation: altitude, distance, speed or fuel calculations.
  • Legal and regulatory compliance where specific units or factors are required.
  • Money: currency conversion, taxes or any financial operation (the app doesn't convert currencies).
  • Scientific, laboratory or industrial work that needs traceable, certified measurements, or that involves hazardous materials.

In these cases, use authoritative sources, calibrated instruments and qualified professionals.

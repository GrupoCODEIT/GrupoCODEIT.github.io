Convertify — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026 · App: Convertify (package com.grupocodeit.convertifyapp)

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in. Your conversions, favorites and settings stay on your phone (and in your Android backup, if it's on). We never receive them.
  • The app is free and shows ads. Google's ad, analytics and crash-reporting tools collect some device data, such as your Advertising ID and an approximate location based on your IP address.
  • The app tells Firebase Analytics which features you use, such as the category and units of a conversion. It never sends the numbers you type or the results.
  • Watching one rewarded ad unlocks a premium feature until midnight. No payment, no account.
  • Where consent is required (for example, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you're asked before any ad is requested, analytics follows your choice, and you can change it in Settings > Privacy Choices.
  • We don't sell your data.

Who we are

Convertify is developed by GRUPO CODEIT ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your data: grupocodeit.business@gmail.com. Website: grupocodeit.github.io.

What the app keeps on your device

The app has no servers of its own. It keeps the following in its private storage on your phone, and we never receive it:

  • Conversion history. Every conversion you confirm is saved with its category, the units, the value you entered, the result, the formula, the number of decimal places and the date and time. It's saved even while the History feature is locked, so it's there when you unlock it.
  • Favorites: the unit pairs you save (category, units and the date you saved them).
  • Settings: real-time conversion on or off, decimal places, number format, default category, language and theme.
  • Premium status: which features you've unlocked and when they expire. For each one, the app stores the date and time you unlocked it, plus a reading of the phone's internal clock, which counts the time since the phone was turned on, so the app can tell if the clock has been changed.
  • Your consent choices, saved by Google's consent tool so you aren't asked every time.
  • Exported files. The CSV and PDF files you export are created in the app's private storage, and you choose where to send them from Android's share menu. We don't receive them.

Deleting your data

  • In History, you can delete one conversion or clear them all. Settings > Clear History and Settings > Clear Favorites do the same for all your history or favorites.
  • Clearing the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > Convertify > Storage) or uninstalling the app deletes everything the app keeps on your phone, including exported files.

Backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may save the app's data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. A direct transfer between phones can also move it. This backup is managed by Google under your account; we can't access it.

  • Included: your history, favorites, settings and exported files.
  • Left out: your premium status and the record Google's consent tool keeps of your choices. So premium never moves to another phone, and where consent is required, you're asked again.
  • Firebase and the ads SDK keep their own files, which can include a copy of your consent signals and identifiers such as the app instance ID. These files may be included in the backup and restored.

Analytics events

Besides what Firebase collects automatically, the app records events such as: screens you open; conversions (category and units); use of multi-conversion and conversion chains (category, unit and how many results or steps); favorites and history actions; exports (format and number of records); premium unlocks (which feature, and whether the ad was shown); ads shown or not available; the consent flow (whether ads can be requested, and error codes); and which setting you changed. The app also stores your settings (real-time mode, decimal places, default category, theme, number format and language) as analytics user properties, and adds the app version to every event.

The numbers you type, the results, your search text and your saved data are never sent. Crash and error reports describe the technical problem; when a conversion or an export fails, they may include its category, units or file format, never the values. Crash reports can also include the analytics events recorded just before the crash.

Advertising ID

Google's ad and analytics tools use your device's Advertising ID to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can delete or reset it anytime on Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device). After you delete it, apps can no longer use it for personalized ads. The ads SDK also reads an app set ID, an identifier that Google Play services gives to apps from the same developer; deleting or resetting your Advertising ID doesn't change it.

Approximate location

The app doesn't ask for location permission and doesn't use GPS. Google's services may infer an approximate location (such as your country or city) from your IP address, for ad delivery, analytics, fraud prevention and to tell whether consent is required where you are.

Consent

  • If you're in a region where consent is required (for example, the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form. No ads are requested until Google's consent tool confirms they can be, and until your choice is known, the app sets all Firebase Analytics consent types to denied.
  • If the app can't reach Google's consent tool before you've made a choice, it requests no ads and denies all analytics consent types. If you've already chosen, it uses the choice saved on your phone.
  • If you decline, the app still works. Ads may be limited or non-personalized, as Google defines them, or unavailable, in which case premium features can't be unlocked.
  • You can change your choice anytime in Settings > Privacy Choices (shown where it applies to your region).
  • Firebase Analytics follows your choice through Google Consent Mode v2. When consent is denied, Google may still receive limited, cookieless signals without identifiers, as described in Google's policies. Where consent isn't required, analytics is allowed by default; the Advertising ID controls above still apply.
  • Crash reporting isn't tied to consent: we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working. It can't be turned off inside the app; uninstalling the app stops it.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent: personalized ads, storing and reading information on your device for ads, and analytics storage. You give or refuse it in Google's consent form and can change it in Privacy Choices.
  • Legitimate interest: crash reporting, fraud prevention and security, showing non-personalized or limited ads that keep the app free, and the limited analytics signals without identifiers that Google may still receive when you decline (see Consent).
  • Legal obligation: keeping a record of your consent choices.

Premium until midnight

Single conversions are free. Some features are premium: multi-conversion, history, favorites, conversion chains, CSV and PDF export, dark mode and no ads. Watching one rewarded ad unlocks the feature you chose (or, with the Full Premium Pass, every premium feature) until midnight, your local time, on the day you watch it. It ends at midnight, not after 24 hours. A feature is unlocked only when Google's ad SDK confirms you earned the reward, usually by watching the ad to the end. The app stores which features are unlocked and when they expire, only on your phone: premium isn't included in backups. If the phone's date changes, premium ends, and changing the time zone or setting the clock back can end it too. The next day, you can watch another ad.

Who receives your data

We don't sell your data. Google (AdMob, User Messaging Platform, Firebase) collects the data described above to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, provide analytics and report crashes, and processes it under its own policies. Through Google's ad auctions, other advertisers and ad technology companies that buy or measure ads through Google may also receive data from ad requests (such as your Advertising ID, IP address and device info, as your consent choices allow); where consent is required, Google's consent form lists them. Google may process this data in the United States and other countries; for data from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and on Standard Contractual Clauses. When you export a file, you choose where it goes. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply.

If you live in a US state with a privacy law (such as California), personalized ads may count as "selling" or "sharing" your data for targeted advertising. You can opt out by deleting your Advertising ID in your Android settings (on older Android versions, by opting out of ads personalization there); see Advertising ID above.

Retention

  • Your history, favorites, settings and exported files stay until you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Premium features end at midnight. The record of when you unlocked them stays until you unlock them again, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Your consent choices stay until you change them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • If Android backup is on, the backup copy stays in your Google account under Google's backup rules, even after you uninstall the app.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as we need them to answer you.
  • Data collected by Google is kept under Google's own retention policies: user-level analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, and aggregated reports that don't identify you may be kept longer; crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

Security

Data sent by these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). What the app keeps is stored in its private storage, which other apps can't read. Other apps can read an exported file only if you choose them in the share menu.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you are under the age at which you can consent to data processing in your country, please use the app only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we'll help you clear what the app keeps on the phone and delete or reset the Advertising ID.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, or to withdraw your consent at any time. We don't hold personal data about you on our own servers: the app has no accounts, and what it keeps stays on your phone. In practice, you can delete what the app keeps with the options under Deleting your data, change your ad consent in Privacy Choices, and delete or reset your Advertising ID. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy tools. If you write to us, we'll help as far as we can. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. When we do, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

  • Se incluyen: tu historial, tus favoritos, tus ajustes y los archivos exportados.
  • Quedan fuera: tu estado premium y el registro de tus opciones que guarda la herramienta de consentimiento de Google. Así, premium nunca pasa a otro teléfono y, donde se requiere consentimiento, se te vuelve a preguntar.

Eventos de analítica

Además de lo que Firebase recopila automáticamente, la app registra eventos como: las pantallas que abres; las conversiones (categoría y unidades); el uso de la multiconversión y de las cadenas de conversión (categoría, unidad y cuántos resultados o pasos); acciones en favoritos e historial; exportaciones (formato y cantidad de registros); desbloqueos premium (qué función y si el anuncio se mostró); anuncios mostrados o no disponibles; el flujo de consentimiento (si se pueden solicitar anuncios y códigos de error); y qué ajuste cambiaste. La app también guarda tus ajustes (tiempo real, decimales, categoría predeterminada, tema, formato de números e idioma) como propiedades de usuario de analítica, y agrega la versión de la app a cada evento.

Nunca se envían los números que escribes, los resultados, lo que buscas ni tus datos guardados. Los reportes de fallos y errores describen el problema técnico; cuando falla una conversión o una exportación, pueden incluir su categoría, sus unidades o el formato del archivo, nunca los valores. Los reportes de fallos también pueden incluir los eventos de analítica registrados justo antes del fallo.

ID de publicidad

Las herramientas de anuncios y analítica de Google usan el ID de publicidad de tu dispositivo para mostrar y medir anuncios y prevenir fraude. Puedes borrarlo o restablecerlo cuando quieras en Android: Configuración > Privacidad > Anuncios (o Configuración > Google > Anuncios, según tu dispositivo). Después de borrarlo, las apps ya no pueden usarlo para anuncios personalizados. El SDK de anuncios también lee un ID de conjunto de apps, un identificador que Google Play Services da a las apps de un mismo desarrollador; borrar o restablecer tu ID de publicidad no lo cambia.

Ubicación aproximada

La app no pide permiso de ubicación y no usa GPS. Los servicios de Google pueden deducir una ubicación aproximada (como tu país o ciudad) a partir de tu dirección IP, para mostrar anuncios, analítica, prevención de fraude y para saber si en tu región se requiere consentimiento.

Premium hasta la medianoche

La app guarda qué funciones están desbloqueadas y cuándo vencen, solo en tu teléfono: premium no se incluye en las copias de seguridad. Si cambia la fecha del teléfono, premium termina, y cambiar la zona horaria o atrasar el reloj también puede terminarlo. Al día siguiente puedes ver otro anuncio.
